### Do this before your first upload

**Settings → Channel → Advanced settings → Channel language → English**

This is the switch that later lets you attach a **Hindi audio track** to an English
video. Set it now and you serve both languages from one channel, with all the watch
time compounding in one place. Miss it and you'll end up splitting into two channels.
