# Kids Short — The Little Elephant Who Forgot How to Trumpet

Category 2, Topic #1. Interactive sound-guessing tale. Target: 45–60s per language version.

## English Script

Chintu the elephant woke up one day... and tried to trumpet. "Paaa!" ...nothing came out!

"Oh no," said Chintu. "Where did my trumpet go?"

He asked the lion. "Can you help me roar?" The lion went ROARRR! "That's not it," said Chintu.

He asked the monkey. "Can you help me chatter?" The monkey went chee-chee-chee! "That's not it either!"

Then a tiny bird landed on his trunk. Chintu laughed — hahaha — and... PAAAA-POOO! There it was! His big, happy trumpet sound!

Chintu danced and trumpeted all day long. Sometimes the silliest things... bring back the biggest sounds!

## Hindi Script (हिन्दी)

चिंटू हाथी एक दिन उठा... और आवाज़ निकालने की कोशिश की। "पां!" ...कुछ नहीं हुआ!

"अरे नहीं," चिंटू बोला। "मेरी आवाज़ कहाँ गई?"

उसने शेर से पूछा। "क्या तुम दहाड़ना सिखाओगे?" शेर बोला — गुर्रर्र! "नहीं, ये तो नहीं है," चिंटू बोला।

उसने बंदर से पूछा। "क्या तुम चीं-चीं करना सिखाओगे?" बंदर बोला — ची-ची-ची! "ये भी नहीं!"

तभी एक नन्ही चिड़िया उसकी सूंड पर बैठ गई। चिंटू हंस पड़ा — हाहाहा — और... पां-पूं! वो रही उसकी बड़ी, खुशी वाली आवाज़!

चिंटू दिन भर नाचता और आवाज़ निकालता रहा। कभी-कभी सबसे छोटी बातें... सबसे बड़ी खुशियाँ ले आती हैं!

## Character Bounce Cues (animation notes)

- Chintu: squash-and-stretch on every spoken line (20% vertical squash on emphasis words), ears flap on "trumpet" attempts.
- Each animal interaction: anticipation pose (0.15s) before its vocalization, overshoot on return to idle.
- Scene cut cadence: every 3–5s per style guide, faster (1.5–2.5s) during the final dance/trumpet chorus.
