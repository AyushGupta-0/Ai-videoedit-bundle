Switch on a torch. That light leaves you at three hundred thousand kilometres per second. Now chase it. Run at half the speed of light, and measure the beam again. It is still running away from you at exactly the same speed. That is not a flaw in your rocket. It is a rule the universe is built on. To actually reach light speed, you would need infinite energy. Every engine ever built would not be enough. But push close, ninety nine point nine percent, and reality bends to let you through. The stars ahead squeeze into a tunnel of blue fire. Space itself contracts. A four light year trip shrinks to weeks. And your clock slows down. Fly to Andromeda, and you step out after twenty eight years. Earth would have aged two and a half million. Everyone you knew, gone. Their cities, gone. Light speed was never a speed limit. It is a one way door into the future. And it all starts with something you do without thinking.
