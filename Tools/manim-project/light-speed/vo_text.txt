Switch on a light. That beam leaves you at three hundred thousand kilometres per second. Chase it. And no matter how fast you go, it still runs away from you at exactly the same speed. That is not a limit of engineering. It is a rule built into the universe. But get close, ninety nine point nine nine percent of light speed, and reality starts bending to let you through. The stars ahead crush into a tunnel of blue white light. Space itself contracts. A four light year trip shrinks to a few weeks. And time slows down, for you. Fly to Andromeda, two and a half million light years away, and you would arrive after about twenty eight years of your own life. But Earth would be two and a half million years older. Light speed is not a speed limit. It is a one way door into the future.
