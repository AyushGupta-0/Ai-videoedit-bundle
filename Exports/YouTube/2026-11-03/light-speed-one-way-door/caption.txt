You Can Never Reach Light Speed — But Getting Close Is Stranger

— — — — — — — — — — — — — — —
DESCRIPTION

Chase a beam of light at half the speed of light. It still runs away from you at exactly the same speed.

That isn't a flaw in the rocket. It's a rule the universe is built on.

Push to 99.9% of light speed and reality starts bending to let you through. The stars ahead squeeze into a tunnel. Space in front of you physically contracts — a four light-year trip shrinks to weeks. Your clock slows down.

Fly to Andromeda and you'd step out after 28 years. Earth would have aged two and a half million.

Everyone you knew, gone. Their cities, gone.

Light speed was never a speed limit. It's a one-way door into the future.
