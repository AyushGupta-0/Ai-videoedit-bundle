Light Speed Tak Pahunchna Namumkin Kyun Hai? | Hindi

— — — — — — — — — — — — — — —
DESCRIPTION

Torch on karo. Us light ki speed hai — teen lakh kilometre per second. Ab usko chase karo.

Aadhi light speed pe bhaago, aur dobara naapo. Woh abhi bhi aapse utni hi tezi se door ja rahi hai.

Ye aapke rocket ki kami nahi hai — ye universe ka rule hai.

99.9% light speed pe reality khud raasta de deti hai. Aage ke sitare ek tunnel mein simat jaate hain. Space khud sikud jaata hai. Aur aapki ghadi dheere ho jaati hai.

Andromeda chale jao — aap wahan utróge 28 saal baad. Lekin Earth 25 lakh saal budhi ho chuki hogi.

Light speed kabhi speed limit thi hi nahi. Ye future ka one-way darwaza hai.
