Day 3. Every shelf in the service station is already empty.

But everyone loots the shelves. Nobody loots the plumbing.
40 litres sit in the water heater.

Rule 1: fill from the lowest tap.
Rule 2: never let a door close behind you.
Rule 3: if the lights come on, leave.

2:06 AM. Every light comes on at once.

"Store's reopening. Bring what you've got to the front."

Mains power failed on day one.
Nothing here has electricity.

And the lights are on.

Do you walk to the front? 👇
