Day 5. Your car has eleven miles left, and every road south ends at one bridge.

Rule 1: walk the sleepers, never the ballast.
Rule 2: count bridges, not miles.
Rule 3: never shelter anywhere with one door.

A locomotive. Idling. Nobody in the cab.

4:55 AM. You go into the signal box for the keys. The staircase creaks behind you.

“Driver? That you? Come down.”

All rail staff evacuated on day one. There is no driver.

Then the radio loop plays past where it ever has.

Do you go down the stairs? 👇
