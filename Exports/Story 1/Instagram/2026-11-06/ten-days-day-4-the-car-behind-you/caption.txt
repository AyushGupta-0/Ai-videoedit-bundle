Day 4. Headlights 400 metres back, for sixty miles.
You slow down. They slow down.

Rule 1: never drive straight to where you sleep.
Rule 2: never let a stranger see your fuel.
Rule 3: do not stop on open road.

1:17 AM. You kill your lights and turn off.

Behind you, they stop. And they flash their headlights.
Three long. Three short. Three long.

That's not a threat. That's S.O.S.

And you kept driving.

Thirty seconds later their lights go out — and don't come back on.

Did you just leave someone to die? 👇
