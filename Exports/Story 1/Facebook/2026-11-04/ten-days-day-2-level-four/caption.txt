Day 2. Fourth floor of a car park, because concrete has no windows.

Rule 1: park facing the exit.
Rule 2: never sleep in the driver's seat.
Rule 3: count the cars before dark.

Eleven cars. Yours is the twelfth.

3:40 AM. Someone opens the stairwell door and lets it close softly.

"I saw your brake lights. Which car are you in?"

You haven't touched the brakes since yesterday.

So you count again.
Thirteen.

Do you start the engine? 👇
