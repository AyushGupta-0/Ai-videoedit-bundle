Last night the car park kept you alive. Tonight it costs you something.

Rule 1: park facing the ramp. So you do.
Rule 2: sleep in the back. Never the driver's seat.
Rule 3: count the cars before dark.

Eleven cars. Yours is twelve.

3:40 AM. The stairwell door opens, and someone lets it close softly.

“I know you're on four. I saw your brake lights.”

You coasted in. Nobody saw you.

So you count.
Thirteen.

Do you start the engine? 👇
