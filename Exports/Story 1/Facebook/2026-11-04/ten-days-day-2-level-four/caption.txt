Last night the car park kept you alive. Tonight it costs you something.

Rule 1: park facing the ramp.
Rule 2: sleep in the back seat.
Rule 3: count the cars before dark.

Eleven cars. Yours is the twelfth.

3:40 AM. Someone opens the stairwell door and lets it close softly.

“I know you're on four. I saw your brake lights come on.”

You coasted in. You never touched the brakes.

So you count the cars again.
Thirteen.

Do you start the engine? 👇
