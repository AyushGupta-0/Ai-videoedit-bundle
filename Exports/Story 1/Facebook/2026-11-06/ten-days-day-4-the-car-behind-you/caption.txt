Day 4. An empty A-road, 1 AM. Headlights 400 metres back — for sixty miles.

Rule 1: never drive to where you sleep.
Rule 2: never let a stranger see your fuel.
Rule 3: do not stop on open road.

1:17 AM. You kill your lights and take a junction.

Behind you, they stop. And they flash.
Three long. Three short. Three long.

You keep driving.
In the mirror, their lights don't fade. They go out.

Did you just leave someone to die? 👇
