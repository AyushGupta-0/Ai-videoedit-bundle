Day 10. Antarctica. Minus sixty. They freeze solid in under two minutes. You are, genuinely, the safest person alive.

Rule 1: shelter. ✓
Rule 2: food. ✓
Rule 3: cold. ✓

You switch off the runway lights so nothing can find you from the air. On paper, you have thought of everything.

Then Priya turns your radio over. And asks why a receiver has an aerial.

Day 1 in the traffic. Day 3 in the service station. Day 5 in the signal box. Every time you cranked it to listen, you were transmitting.

The loop was never a broadcast. It was someone answering you.

Outside, the runway lights come back on. By themselves.

Who was on the radio? 👇
